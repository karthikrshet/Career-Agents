# Career Agents Writing & Tone Guidelines (`_writing.md`)

Writing standards for career artifacts:
1. **Action Verbs**: Lead with authoritative verbs (Architected, Engineered, Optimized, Spearheaded). Avoid passive phrases (Responsible for, Assisted with).
2. **Signal-to-Noise**: Keep sentences dense with technical keywords, architectural constraints, and business outcomes.
3. **No Fluff**: Eliminate empty buzzwords ("hardworking team player", "passionate developer").
4. **Tone**: Crisp, professional, engineering-first, and metric-calibrated.
